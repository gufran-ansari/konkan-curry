The Story

Coastal food, kept clear and precise.

Konkan Curry is shaped by the western coast: fresh seafood, coconut, chilli, tamarind, kokum, and the quiet confidence of recipes that do not need to shout.

The identity is seafood-first. Every plate is built around clean freshness, controlled heat, and flavour that lets the catch stay at the centre.

Philosophy

Three rules guide the kitchen.

I

Fresh Catch First

Seafood should taste clean, fresh, and honest.

II

Coastal Spice Balance

Coconut, chilli, tamarind, and heat used with control.

III

Cooked With Intent

No shortcuts, no generic masala overload.

From Kitchen to Table

The craft stays visible.

A measured progression from heat and spice to sauce and plate: each stage is handled for texture, aroma, and restraint.
