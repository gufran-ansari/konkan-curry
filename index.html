Who We Are

Bringing the Heart of
Konkan to Navi Mumbai

At Konkan Curry, we bring the soulful flavours of North India and the West Coast straight to Navi Mumbai. Imagine the smoky aroma of fresh-caught delicacies, the richness of hand-ground masalas, and the comfort of homestyle coastal meals all on your plate.

Our menu is a tribute to legendary coastal food culture, enhanced with a modern dining twist. Whether you’re planning a family gathering or an indulgent weekend feast, Konkan Curry promises a culinary experience that’s as vibrant and flavourful as the shores themselves.
